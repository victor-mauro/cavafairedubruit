ça va faire du bruit. — Victor Mauro, designer

Ça va faire du bruit. explores the sensory dimension of objects and scenographic devices

This approach is meant to be cross-disciplinary. Rich in partnerships, it brings together many skills, from drawing to the creation of sound worlds, by way of interactive technologies.

The name carries a double meaning: “making” noise, designing with one's own hands, while also “making some noise”, sparking surprise and creating an event.
